WHO WE ARE

Our Story

Techlance Global Enterprises was established with a clear vision: to provide reliable engineering, fabrication, manpower, and technical support services to industries where quality, safety, precision, and timely execution matter.

Our experience comes from working closely with engineering and construction teams on challenging industrial projects involving process piping, storage tanks, pressure vessels, structural fabrication, welding, equipment installation, and site execution.

From engineering drawings and material planning to fabrication, welding, inspection, manpower deployment, and site support, we understand the practical requirements of industrial projects. Our approach is focused on combining engineering knowledge with hands-on field experience to deliver practical and dependable solutions.

TECHNICAL EXPERIENCE

Our Pressure Vessel & Tank Experience

A key part of our technical experience is in the fabrication and execution of pressure vessels, process equipment, storage tanks, and related components. Our experience includes working with:

Pressure vessels and process vesselsKnock-Out Drums (KOD)Water Seal DrumsFuel and oil storage tanksHFO Day, Buffer & Storage TanksLarge-diameter vessels and tank structuresShell, nozzle, manhole and ladder fabricationPiping connections and vessel attachmentsWelding and fabrication of carbon steel componentsInspection, NDT coordination and quality documentation

Our project exposure includes large fabricated equipment with significant shell thicknesses and dimensions, requiring controlled welding procedures, fit-up, alignment, inspection and coordinated site execution.
